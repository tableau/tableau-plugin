# Dashboard documentation

Adapt sections to the target and reader; do not fill irrelevant sections.

## Brief

1. title and scope;
2. purpose, intended reader, and decision supported;
3. key visible metrics with confirmed or qualified definitions;
4. visible filters/context and what is known about their scope;
5. how to read the first view;
6. data-through/update context and important caveats.

## Detailed

1. **Identity and ownership:** workbook/view/project and maintained-by contact only when useful and observable.
2. **Purpose and decisions:** confirmed intent first; clearly label inferred purpose/audience.
3. **Metric guide:** display label, business definition, calculation/source field when verified, aggregation/grain, favorable direction when known, units, and caveats.
4. **Views and charts:** visible chart purpose, encodings, comparisons, axes, reference lines, annotations, and limitations.
5. **Filters and interactions:** visible controls, defaults in the rendered state, verified action behavior, reset/clearing behavior, and unknowns.
6. **Usage paths:** question → starting element → next view/filter → interpretation; avoid inventing thresholds or actions.
7. **Data context:** datasource, scope, grain, time coverage, update timestamp/cadence only when known, exclusions, and permission caveats.
8. **Glossary:** only domain terms the reader needs.
9. **Coverage/validation:** evidence used, unverified definitions, and owner confirmations required.

## Design observations

Include only when requested or useful for documentation maintenance. Keep it short and unscored: visible hierarchy, legibility at the supplied render, color reliance, and potentially misleading visible framing. Do not claim WCAG conformance from an image alone or turn this into a full critique.
